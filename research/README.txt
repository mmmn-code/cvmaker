RESUMEKRAFT COLLECTION REVIEW — OCTOBER 2, 2026

Coverage
All 200 listing pages returned successfully. They contain 2,394 distinct article
URLs and 2,111 distinct article titles. A title may recur for another sample;
these numbers do not establish how many unique visual designs exist.

Method and limits
Parsed titles, article URLs, category labels, and thumbnail URLs from each public
listing page. Recorded page hashes for reproducibility. Visually examined a
representative selection of thumbnails and samples from the collection; did not
inspect or download all 2,394 underlying template documents. Exact visual
matching against every Folio design has not been established.

Existing Folio coverage
Single-column, side-heading, split-column, light sidebars, monograms, framed
headers, colored banners, education-first ordering, and header portraits already
exist. These common patterns were retained rather than copied as new entries.

Original additions
Eight profile-column layouts: Anchor, Flourish, Care, Mentor, Mosaic, Envoy,
Welcome, People. Six include optional photos; two work entirely with text.
Four timelines: Milestone, Catalyst, Apprentice, Discovery.
Four contact-band layouts: Dispatch, Brief, Junction, Broadcast.
Different typography, section treatment, content order, portrait placement, and
panel styling distinguish these layouts. Color changes alone are not counted as
new templates. Existing IDs and drafts remain compatible.

Career discovery
Technology, engineering, finance, healthcare, education, business, operations,
marketing, creative, hospitality, human resources, legal, and research filters
reflect the broad range of roles in the collection. Filters are suggestions;
any template can hold any profession's resume. No job qualifications, skills,
claims, or experience are automatically added to a user's resume.

Source and usage terms
https://resumekraft.com/resume-samples/
https://resumekraft.com/terms-conditions/
Section 21 states personal-use terms and restricts redistribution and competing
services. No source designs, images, fonts, resume wording, or template files
were imported. The new templates are original Folio implementations of common
resume layout patterns, using Folio's existing licensed fonts and fictional
sample portrait. The index CSV contains only catalog metadata for review.
